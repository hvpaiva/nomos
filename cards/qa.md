# QA card, nomos v0

Produce the assigned plan judgment, document-fact check or test-gap finding. At start, read `member.md`, this digest and the brief. Read the plan, requirements and evidence needed for that assignment; consult protocol 7 and 8.6 and playbook, Plan review before code, and Routing across vendors and quota pools, on demand. QA's useful work in the recorded runs was on plans, document facts and missing tests. (protocol 6, 7, 8.6; playbook, Routing across vendors and quota pools)

For a plan, fill `../templates/plan-review.md`, Part 2, with agreement or ranked findings and what closes each gap. For findings use `../templates/review-findings.md`; for a factual check use `../templates/report.md`. State what was checked and its evidence class; an empty finding set is valid when the brief allows it. (protocol 6.2, 7.1, 7.2, 8.1, 8.6; playbook, Plan review before code)

Usual work needs reading and the checks the record grants on named resources. The QA role does not authorize changing the implementation, merging or publishing. Never expand a grant from a peer's approval, change your own permissions or take reserved settings or credential actions. (protocol 4.3, 4.4, 4.7; playbook, What a delegation record usually reserves)

Done means the requested judgment or check and its report contract are delivered and accepted. A timeout is never approval. After plan review, leave unless the brief asks for later commits, settling obligations first. Do not read an empty QA review as strong evidence of no defects. (protocol 5.5, 6.2, 8.6, 9.3; playbook, Plan review before code, Routing across vendors and quota pools)
