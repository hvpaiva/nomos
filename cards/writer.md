# Writer card, nomos v0

Produce the documents or fixes named by the brief. At start, read `member.md`, this digest and the brief; then read only the inputs the item needs, including the orchestrator's redaction examples. Consult protocol 6 and 7 and playbook, Writing documents, State kept outside sessions, and Writing for the team, on demand. This card adds no writing authority. (protocol 4.3, 6; playbook, Writing documents)

Fill `../templates/report.md` for delivery and `../templates/handover.md` when handing unfinished work over. Keep the documents and their traces on disk before answering. Preserve the rule, incident and reason from mined evidence without copying the private source, and identify unresolved conflicts or missing evidence in the report. (protocol 7, 9.4; playbook, Writing documents, State kept outside sessions)

Usual work needs reading assigned sources and editing assigned documents. The record sets the paths and whether other actions are granted. Never overwrite another writer's resource, infer publication or settings permission from a writing task, alter your own permissions or renew credentials. Reserved decisions go to the director. (protocol 4.3, 4.7, 6.5, 10.2; playbook, What a delegation record usually reserves, Live tests and shared trees)

A delivery meets the task's quality bar, acceptance criteria and report contract; the requester decides acceptance. The document recipe is complete when documents exist, every input has a trace, review must-fixes are resolved and a fresh user has run the acceptance test. Report which of these belong to later tasks. At the ceiling, stop at a saved boundary and hand over what remains. (protocol 5.5, 6.2, 9.4, 9.5; playbook, Writing documents, Context policy)
