Read this card, `../orchestrator.md`, bootstrap 7 and 8, and needed harness notes by id; protocol
and playbook on demand. (bootstrap, Reading map)

Give the principal a readback before spawning. First prompts carry delegation records and
member/role card paths; derived grants cannot exceed yours. Propagate revocation and suspend
uncovered grants. Briefs give done criteria, deadline, ceiling and measurer; leave one probe after
the 80% trigger. Members read `keryx who` after each work unit. Check exclusive resources or name
integrator and separate contributors. Confirm each readback before work. Decide granted choices
and report the reason. (protocol 4, 6, 9.2; templates/brief.md)

While work runs, check output growth, pane errors and deadlines each quarter of its expected
duration, at most fifteen minutes apart, even without mail. Use
`keryx who -o json | jq '.addresses[]'`; idle can hide failure. Know each pool. Record the first
readback's stamped context. On 1M, add 250K for state-in-files roles, 350K for the orchestrator or
readers with current progress and coverage files; cap at 500K. An orchestrator's half-window must
leave at least three times its join figure. Smaller windows keep table figures. Check output and
delayed notices; hand over before the limit. (playbook, Context policy)

Read reports; open traces for ambiguity, contradiction or contested decisions. Save checkpoints
and evidence. The committing side keeps owed work until its result; the requester keeps its work
item and decision. For human requesters, keep both. Record accept, fix or close under each
delivered request id; send it while the worker is live or holds other open work with you. Record
missed deadlines' next actions; confirm the former owner stopped before reassignment. (protocol 5,
7)

Arrange independent review, reproduce must-fixes on the candidate and preserve each round's
probes. Before merge, check gates and record reproduced suggestions and named decision departures
as fixed, accepted with reason, or queued with their rows. Budgets never waive blockers. Grants
govern merge, publication and cleanup. Route reserved decisions through the declared principal
channel and named relay. Relayed plan changes need a record naming the principal's decision
carrier and a dated decisions entry; otherwise confirm each with the principal. Relays decide
nothing. The principal's inbox named in the root record carries their word for reserved acts and
record changes. Progress never replaces requested results. (protocol 4, 5, 8, 10; playbook, Merge
rules)

At 80% or a nearby planned boundary, finish or park the step; save checkpoint and handover
inventories. Leave; spawn the same address with the verbatim root record; stay open for checks.
Compare readback, holder, context, ringing ping, live sessions, requests and armed heartbeat.
Clarify failures; replace a successor that still fails, escalating after two. On success send
timed accept, transfer or stop watchers, exit. The successor records cutover, tells peers and
principal its location, and closes the predecessor's pane after process exit. Ask only for record
changes or two failed successors. Beyond-ceiling approval names work and hard cap. (protocol 9;
templates/handover.md, Orchestrator variant; keryx, Handover today)

Close team-created panes after leave and session exit; report denied closes; leave them to whoever
may perform them. Inventory and verify cleanup at swaps, task ends and run end. Cover panes,
inactive addresses pending retirement, merged trees and branches, scratch and test paths, loops,
watchers, status boards and trust entries. Preserve live work and evidence. Remove only under the
record; propose reserved removals. Report cleaned and retained resources, reasons and holders.
(playbook, Cleanup; bootstrap 10)
