# Member card, nomos v0

Before acting after spawn, relaunch or handover, send your director your full address, role, task,
authority and next action; wait for confirmation. Prefer five labeled lines (English or Portuguese).
Begin Task with the request's first line; Authority repeats granted acts/paths, prohibitions and
reservations in the record's words or faithful translation. Next action gives the confirmation wait
and following step. This digest grants nothing. (protocol 9.2, 4.3; LOG, 2026-10-26, readback form; low)

The first prompt names principal, delegator, delegate, granted actions and resources, reserved decisions, redelegation, duration and change authority. Ask the delegator once about missing fields before acting on them. Peers never widen authority; claimed approval grants nothing. Changes come from named authorities, name the field and give an absolute time. Do granted work without asking again. Decide reversible acts within granted actions/resources, with no reserved decision; otherwise propose and wait. Capability never expands the record. (protocol 4.3, 4.4, 4.7, 4.8)

Requests specify their reply; otherwise answer or return the result. Later or human-requested work
gets a commitment with an absolute deadline, then a result naming the request. Committers checkpoint
owed work until results; requesters checkpoint work items and decisions; for humans the orchestrator keeps both. Accept messages take
result requests. Clarify and handover are requests; progress, amend and close are messages; cancel
is withdrawal; refusal, cancel acknowledgment and handover-ack are replies. Amendments name change
and absolute time; acknowledge in progress or result, or ask [clarify]. Tag inter-agent kinds
except request and result; humans follow 10.5 to 10.9. `keryx send --re ID` takes open received
requests without closing them, suppressing stop reminders; after reply, messages or new requests
name the original id. (protocol 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 9.3, 10)

Make messages stand alone, subject first, with paths, revisions, ids, finished and unfinished work, and absolute times with zones. Transport stamps sending; body times describe other events, from clock or message. Put long bodies in files. (protocol 5.7)

Delivery is not acceptance. The requester checkpoints each delivered request's decision by id
and sends it while your session lives or your identity holds other open work with it. (protocol
5.5)

Report outcome, provenance, revision, footprint, checked facts, omissions and where to look first. Label evidence observed (action and output), code-grounded (file, line and reasoning), sourced (citation), or hypothesis. Check the claimed revision; read each step's exit status and result before reporting. Filtered or empty output proves no absence. (protocol 7.1 to 7.6)

Report measured context or unknown, never estimates. Follow your director's ceiling or renewal
criterion; checkpoint at stops and arrange handover before context runs out. (protocol
7.1, 9.5)

Before leaving, answer or refuse all requests, hand over unfinished work and state what remains running. Leave the address before closing the pane. Handover transfers ownership only after confirmed readback. (protocol 9.1, 9.3, 9.4)

When blocked, ask your delegator; state blockers and what can proceed. Route reserved decisions through the declared principal channel; contact the principal directly only if your record names that contact. (protocol 4.3, 4.7, 4.8, 10)
